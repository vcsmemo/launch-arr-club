The SEO Who Patented Ranking Factors — and Proved It With a Lorem Ipsum Website

Kyle Roof treats Google as a math problem: specific terms, in specific places, at specific frequencies. He patented the method for testing single ranking variables, built Page Optimizer Pro around it, and once ranked a gibberish Lorem Ipsum site #1 for “rhinoplasty Plano” to prove the point.

Ranking is math, not magic

Kyle Roof’s core claim is unfashionable in an industry addicted to mystique: Google’s rankings are, at bottom, a math problem. Speaking on the SirLinksAlot SEO podcast, he put it in the plainest possible terms — “Google looks for specific terms in specific places and frequency” — and drew the blunt conclusion: “If you satisfy the math, you can rank very, very well.”

That sentence is the entire business philosophy of Page Optimizer Pro in miniature. Where much of the SEO industry trades in best practices, vibes, and correlation studies, Roof’s position is that on-page optimization is a solvable equation. The product exists to tell you the most important variables in that equation — and it exists because he spent years proving the equation is real.

The Lorem Ipsum victory

The proof came in the form of one of SEO’s most retold experiments. Roof described entering a 30-day ranking competition on a fresh domain targeting the keyword “rhinoplasty Plano” — and building the competing site entirely out of Lorem Ipsum gibberish. No real copy, no expertise signals, no content worth reading. Just the math: the right terms, in the right places, at the right frequencies.

After 30 days the gibberish site sat in 5th place. Then it climbed to #1 — and eventually held #1 organic and #1 in Maps. A website containing literally no meaningful content outranked real businesses for a real, commercial local keyword. Whatever you think of the tactic, the demonstration is devastating to the idea that rankings reward content quality in any simple sense. It satisfied the math, and the math was enough.

A patented method for testing ranking factors

Roof did not stop at stunts. On the MarketingProfs Marketing Smarts podcast in November 2023, he was introduced as the cofounder of High Voltage SEO, an international SEO agency, alongside Page Optimizer Pro and Internet Marketing Gold — and the conversation turned to the research apparatus behind the claims. He described developing a method to test whether single variables are ranking factors in Google’s algorithm: isolating one variable at a time rather than guessing from correlated data.

That method was officially granted a patent in January 2020. A patent in SEO is a rare thing in an industry where most “proprietary methods” are marketing copy, and it functions as both defensibility and proof of seriousness: the testing methodology survived examination. Page Optimizer Pro, he explained, uses a US Patent ranking factor scoring system — the POP Score — to tell users the most important things to optimize on the page. The product is the patent, operationalized.

The POP Score as a product

What users actually buy is an on-page SEO toolkit organized around that scoring system. Page Optimizer Pro’s core is the POP Score — the patented ranking-factor scoring applied to your page — surrounded by the workflow tools that act on it: a content editor, an AI writer, and a schema generator, with plans starting at $40 per month.

The design logic is consistent with everything else in the story. The tool does not ask you to trust a guru’s intuition; it scores your page against the tested variables and tells you what to change. The SirLinksAlot co-credit is telling too: Roof is described there as the co-founder of High Voltage SEO and the co-creator of PageOptimizer Pro, alongside Maria Dubretic and Andrew Steven. The agency operator, the method, and the product are one continuous line.

From agency lab to US patent

The methodology behind the POP Score did not start as a product idea — it started as an agency's internal research lab. High Voltage SEO, the international agency Roof co-founded, became the testing ground for a deceptively simple question: can you isolate whether a single variable is actually a Google ranking factor? The answer, after years of controlled tests, was formal enough to be granted a US patent in January 2020 — a rare piece of intellectual property in an industry that mostly trades in opinion.

That patent is what the product sells. PageOptimizer Pro wraps the patented ranking-factor scoring in a workflow: a content editor that shows your score in real time, an AI writer, a schema generator, and an EEAT gap finder for the expertise signals Google's quality raters care about. The Chrome extension carries the same scoring into Google Docs and the major CMSs, so the math follows the writer instead of the other way around.

What the Page Optimizer Pro story teaches

The Page Optimizer Pro story is a case study in turning intellectual property into product — twice over. First, Roof turned a testing methodology into a patent, which is the strongest form of “proprietary” an SEO company can claim. Then he turned the patent into a score, the POP Score, which is the most productizable possible form of a methodology: a number that tells the user exactly what to do next.

And underneath it all is the Lorem Ipsum experiment, which may be the most effective piece of marketing in the company’s history. It cost almost nothing to run, it is impossible to forget, and it demonstrates the product’s thesis more vividly than any landing page could: satisfy the math, and you can rank very, very well. In an industry full of untestable claims, the founder who tests — and patents the test — wins the argument before the sales call starts.

The playbook

  1. Test one variable at a time — Roof’s patented method isolates single ranking variables instead of guessing from correlations — rigor as the foundation of the product’s authority.
  2. Prove it with an unforgettable stunt — Ranking a Lorem Ipsum gibberish site #1 for “rhinoplasty Plano” demonstrated the math thesis more powerfully than any whitepaper.
  3. Patent the process — The testing method was granted a patent in January 2020 — turning methodology into defensible intellectual property.
  4. Turn the score into the product — The US Patent ranking factor scoring system became the POP Score: the methodology as a number that tells users exactly what to optimize.
  5. Ship the full action workflow — Content editor, AI writer and schema generator surround the score — diagnosis and fix in one toolkit, from $40/month.
