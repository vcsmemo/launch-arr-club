What AI Toolbox is about

What makes it different

AI Toolbox (formerly ChatGPT Toolbox) is a Chrome extension that adds nested folders, full-text search across every message, bookmarks, a prompt library and bulk export to ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude and Grok — 40,000+ users, 4.7/5 Chrome Web Store rating. Version 3.0 launched in September 2026 and won #1 Product of the Day on Product Hunt. Founded by Adi Leviim (product & frontend) and Mohammad El-Esawi (backend & LLM systems). Roughly 75% of revenue comes from one-time lifetime plans ($99/platform, $199 all-access), so 30-day revenue is the honest metric — the subscription component is about $5.7k MRR.
