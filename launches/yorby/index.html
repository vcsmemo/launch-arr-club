What Yorby is about

What makes it different

Yorby is an AI toolkit for going viral on social media, built by creators Andrew Meng and Thomas Kim. It writes short-form scripts, remixes viral formats into your niche, and watches accounts to alert you when a video starts taking off.

After a viral spike took Yorby from ~$6k to $11.5k MRR, churn settled it at a $9.4k MRR plateau — which co-founder Thomas Kim documented in his own revenue update video. Plans from $40/mo.
