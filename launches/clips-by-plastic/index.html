What Clips by Plastic is about

What makes it different

Drop in a finished song and your own footage or photos, and Clips finds the tempo and every part of the song — verses, pre-chorus, chorus, bridge — then makes a vertical lyric video for each one, cut on the beat with the lyrics synced.

Pick a layout and one of 54 effects, then export every part on every backdrop at once, named and ready for Ads Manager. Run them all for a week and put your budget behind the part that wins. Clips works offline on Mac, is free to try, and costs $99 once.
