What Inkwell AI is about

Who is it for?

Solo founders and small marketing teams who publish content every week but don't have a full-time writer.

What problem does it solve?

Writing in a consistent brand voice takes hours every week, and generic AI tools produce text that sounds like everyone else.

What job does it do?

It drafts blog posts, newsletters and landing pages in your own voice from a rough bullet list.

How does it solve it?

You connect your past writing; Inkwell builds a voice profile and generates drafts you edit instead of starting from a blank page.

What makes it different?

Unlike generic AI writers, it scores every draft against your voice profile — if it doesn't sound like you, it rewrites itself.
