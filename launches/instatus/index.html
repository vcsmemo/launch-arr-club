What Instatus is about

What makes it different

Instatus lets companies create beautiful status pages in seconds: monitor services, fix incidents with the team, and share status with customers. It pairs the status page with uptime monitoring, real-time alerts across channels, and integrations with Slack and Teams.

A free-forever plan and 'takes 30 seconds, no card required' onboarding make it the indie, bootstrapped alternative to Atlassian Statuspage.

The builder is Ali Salah, a solo founder and developer from Egypt, who quit his job to create Instatus — a story Starter Story has now featured twice, original interview and five-year follow-up.

The revenue figure is self-reported by the founder on the ARRFounder founder directory: $50K MRR, re-crawled and re-confirmed by the IdeaWave revenue tracker every morning since Aug 1, 2026 — most recently on Oct 14, 2026, when Instatus ranked #88 of 290 tracked startups.
