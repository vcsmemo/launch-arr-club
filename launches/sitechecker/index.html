What Sitechecker is about

What makes it different

Sitechecker is an all-in-one SEO platform for monitoring and optimizing websites: it crawls for technical issues, checks on-page SEO, tracks keyword rankings across desktop, mobile, and local results, monitors backlinks, and plugs into Google Search Console and GA4 to surface SEO metrics in one dashboard.

It also tracks AI visibility — how and where a brand is cited by AI assistants in the same prompts — and is popular with freelancers and agencies, claiming 85,000+ SEO professionals use it.
