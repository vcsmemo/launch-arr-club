What Bazzly is about

What makes it different

Bazzly is an AI blogger tool built to help businesses research, plan, write, publish, and continuously optimize SEO content that earns more organic traffic.

Unlike typical AI writers, it starts from your Search Console data — analyzing real search demand, building topical authority maps, and generating full articles with cover images, FAQ, schema markup and internal links.

You approve every title; nothing publishes without your say-so. Auto-publishes to WordPress, Webflow, Shopify, Ghost or any webhook.
