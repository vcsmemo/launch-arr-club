What PixelPilot is about

Who it is for

Indie makers and junior designers who don't have a senior designer to review their work.

The problem

Getting honest, specific design critique is slow — friends say "looks good" and Dribbble is a popularity contest.

Job to be done

Upload a mockup, get annotated feedback on spacing, contrast, hierarchy and copy in 30 seconds.

How it solves it

A vision model trained on design-system rules critiques your screenshot and draws directly on it.

What makes it different

It explains the <em>why</em> behind every note and links the rule it applied — it's a tutor, not a judge.
