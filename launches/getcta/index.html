What getcta is about

Who it is for

Creators, founders, and presenters who record videos or present on camera and want a discreet teleprompter.

The problem

Reading scripts on camera looks unnatural, and bulky teleprompter setups are expensive and obvious.

Job to be done

When I record or present, I want my script to follow my voice invisibly so I look natural on camera.

How it solves it

getcta sits under the notch below the camera, auto-scrolling via on-device speech recognition, hidden from screen shares.

What makes it different

Lives in the notch area itself — invisible to viewers and screen recordings, with AI script assistance built in.
