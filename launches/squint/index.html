What Squint is about

Who it is for

Anyone who wants instant AI explanations of anything visible on their screen without screenshots or app-switching.

The problem

Asking AI about something on screen means taking screenshots, switching apps, and uploading images.

Job to be done

When I see something on screen I don't understand, I want an instant AI explanation without breaking flow.

How it solves it

Press a hotkey, drag a box over any screen region, and Squint streams an AI explanation beside it.

What makes it different

Zero-friction screen queries — no screenshots to upload, no app switching, stays out of your way.
