What TaskFlow AI is about

Who it is for

Remote teams of 5–50 whose real work happens in Slack and Discord, not in a project tool.

The problem

Action items get buried in chat threads; standups become archaeology digs through yesterday's messages.

Job to be done

It watches your team channels, extracts tasks, assigns owners, and files them into your board.

How it solves it

A bot joins your channels, uses an LLM to detect commitments and deadlines, and syncs to Linear, Asana or Notion.

What makes it different

It doesn't ask your team to change tools — it meets them where the conversation already happens.
