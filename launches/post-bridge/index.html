What Post Bridge is about

What makes it different

Post Bridge is an all-in-one social media scheduler: write once, publish to ten platforms — X, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Bluesky, Threads and Google Business — from one dashboard, with per-platform caption overrides, a calendar, bulk upload and analytics. Unlimited posts come on every paid plan, and a REST API, an npm CLI and a hosted MCP server let AI agents like Claude and ChatGPT schedule posts on your behalf — Post Bridge is listed in the Claude connector directory and as a ChatGPT plugin.

It is the work of Jack Friks, a Canadian solo founder who was spending about an hour a day manually re-posting his own short-form videos to grow Curiosity Quench, his consumer app that passed 100,000 downloads. He built Post Bridge in about a month, launched it in October 2024 with a hard paywall and no free tier, and still answers every support email himself. In spring 2025 he publicly turned down a $1 million acquisition offer — and has also turned down Y Combinator — staying bootstrapped with no outside investors beyond family support.

The numbers compounded slowly, then fast: roughly $6,000–$7,000 MRR in the first months, about $35,000 by mid-2026, and approximately $48,500 MRR by mid-November 2026, with roughly 1,600 active subscriptions and about $582,000 in all-time revenue — past $500,000 in annualised revenue. Infrastructure costs run under $2,000 a month against $35,000+ MRR, for gross margins above 90%. Plans start at $39 a month for 15 connected accounts.
