What Capgo is about

Who it is for

Teams shipping mobile apps with Capacitor.

The problem

Urgent bug fixes get stuck in App Store / Play Store review queues for hours or days.

Job to be done

When I ship a critical fix, I want users updated instantly without waiting on store review.

How it solves it

Capgo pushes live updates to your Capacitor app over the air — users get the fix on next launch.

What makes it different

OTA updates purpose-built for Capacitor, with channels and rollback for safe rollouts.
