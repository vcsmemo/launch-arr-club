What Ace is about

Who it is for

Operations teams and enterprises that want an AI teammate handling real workflows across their existing tools.

The problem

Knowledge workers lose hours to repetitive cross-app tasks that span Slack, email, and internal tools.

Job to be done

When I have recurring operational work, I want an AI teammate that does it end-to-end so my team focuses on higher-value work.

How it solves it

Ace gets its own Mac, email, and company accounts, then works across your apps 24/7, managed by Automat's team.

What makes it different

Unlike chatbots, Ace is a fully managed agentic employee with its own computer — not a prompt window.
