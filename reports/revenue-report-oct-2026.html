The headline numbers

We pulled together publicly shared revenue data for 20 AI startups — from indie hackers to VC-backed teams. The total: $1.88 million in monthly/30-day revenue. The median product does $63k MRR.

That's not a typo. The median AI startup on our board is doing $63k/month. The AI gold rush is real — but it's not where most people think.

💡 Insight: The top 3 products ($500k, $261k, $184k) account for $945k — over 50% of total tracked revenue. AI revenue follows a power law, just like everything else in startups.

Dev tools punch above their weight

4 dev tools made the list (LLM Gateway $79k, Web3Forms $41k, Capgo $31k, Comp AI $12k). Developers pay for tools that save them time — and they pay consistently. The dev-tools category has the highest revenue-per-product ratio after marketing.

What this means if you're building

1. Pick a category where people already pay. Marketing, dev tools, and sales — these are the categories where AI startups are actually generating revenue. Not "AI girlfriends," not generic chatbots.

2. $63k MRR is the bar. That's the median. If you're building an AI product, that's the number to beat. It's achievable — these aren't OpenAI-scale companies.

3. Revenue transparency is a superpower. Every product on this list shared their numbers publicly. That transparency builds trust with customers, investors, and the community. It's why we built launch.arr.club around revenue, not upvotes.
