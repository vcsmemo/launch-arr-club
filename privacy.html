Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

What we collect

  • Google sign-in: your name, email address, and profile photo — only to identify your submissions. We use the standard openid / email / profile scopes and nothing else.
  • Launch submissions: the product information you provide (name, URL, tagline, description, screenshots, etc.). Published listings are public by design.
  • Revenue data (optional): if you self-report revenue, the numbers you enter; if you connect Stripe, a read-only restricted key used solely to sync aggregate MRR. You can disconnect at any time, which deletes the key immediately. We never see or store your customers' details.

How we use it

  • To publish and display your launch listing.
  • To sync revenue data you explicitly shared to ARR.Club for long-term tracking.
  • To contact you about your submission (e.g., verification, featuring).

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it with third parties except the infrastructure providers that host this site (Cloudflare) and services you explicitly connect (Google, Stripe).
